## About

Elan Ding is a Senior Data Scientist in AI and Computer Vision at LexisNexis Risk Solutions, specializing in property risk modeling and location-based risk analytics. He combines deep learning, computer vision, and actuarial methods to develop innovative solutions for insurance risk assessment.

Elan is also a named inventor on patented approaches in property analytics, including square footage estimation from aerial imagery and deep learning-based risk segmentation. He currently helps lead property risk modeling and AI and Computer Vision research at LexisNexis Risk Solutions, supporting several core risk analytics initiatives.
